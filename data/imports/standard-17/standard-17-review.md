# Standard 17 — Waste management (mandatory standard)

The Health Facility has a hospital/healthcare facility waste management plan that complies to national and local policies

Function 2; Component 4 (Environment).

## 17.1 Healthcare waste management plan

### 17.1.1

The Facility has a waste management plan which is known by clinical and non-clinical staff..

1. Evidence of the facility waste management plan.
2. Evidence of waste management issues discussed through clinical governance committees.
3. If waste management is oursourced, there is a current memorandum of understanding or contract with the external contracter.

## 17.2 Waste and environmental management

### 17.2.1

The Health Facility has approved Guidelines that direct the efficient and sustainable use of energy, water and other utilities.

1. Evidence of recycling, reducing waste and resource conservation.

### 17.2.2

Clinical and other waste are being disposed of in accordance with legislation.

1. Evidence that there are adequate range and supply of bags and waste bins including sharp containers, biohazard bins, general waste bins, and cytotoxic bins.
2. Evidence that waste is being segregated at the source of production.
3. Waste containers are colour- coded and labelled.
4. There are appropriate PPE and equipment for waste management (heavy duty gloves, broom, shovels) in relevant areas.
5. Expired drugs are disposed according to guidelines.
6. Waste disposal area within the facility is fenced and clean.
7. There is a functioning and fenced incinerator in bigger hospitals and other waste management methods in smaller health facilities.
8. Facility clinical and non-clinical staff are aware of protecting against blood and body substances during handling of waste.

## 17.3 Education on waste management

### 17.3.1

Health Facility OICs promote, educate and regularly assess all relevant health workers competencies and compliance in management of healthcare waste and waste management is mandatory in training programs.

1. Evidence of waste management training for clinical and non-clinical staff.
